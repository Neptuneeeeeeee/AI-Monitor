## Bundled runtime in v1.9.4

The application preview embeds CPython 3.13.15 from astral-sh/python-build-standalone release 20260901. Its interpreter and compiled dependencies retain the upstream notice collection in `Contents/Resources/Python/licenses/` and the CPython `LICENSE.txt`. Unused headers, pip, Tk, tests and user packages are not distributed. Files are repackaged and ad-hoc signed for the macOS application.

The public Mozilla trust-root bundle is from certifi 2026.7.22, under MPL-2.0; its license is preserved as `CERTIFI-LICENSE.txt`. No user's private certificate or credential is included. Exact public artifact URLs and SHA-256 values are pinned in `Config/runtime-lock.json` and copied into runtime provenance. Nothing installs or modifies system trust roots.
